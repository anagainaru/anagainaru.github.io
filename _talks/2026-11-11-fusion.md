This talk presents a multi-agent framework that combines domain-specialized agents with independent 
physics-based verification to accelerate materials discovery while maintaining scientific rigor. 

Our manager-orchestrator architecture coordinates specialist agents—each augmented with domain knowledge 
vaults and validated skills—to answer complex research questions. When experimental data is unavailable, 
the system escalates to first-principles calculations; when answers are generated, an independent Correctness 
Agent traces citations to source data and applies drift-detection to predictions. 

Demonstrated through nuclear fuel materials research coordinating TRISO fabrication, fuel performance, and DFT calculation agents, 
the verification framework reduces grading error from 22.5% to 4.3% and detects critical physics errors including 
50× deviations in diffusion models. Across five foundation models, the system improves answer quality by 1.2×-1.5× relative to cost. 
This work provides a generalizable blueprint for trustworthy multi-agent scientific systems.
